## 📌 Overview

**Brightloop** is a subscription management platform designed to help businesses manage customers, products, subscription plans, recurring billing, invoices, and payments in one centralized system.


## ✨ Key Features

- Dashboard and revenue analytics.
- Product and subscription plan management.
- Customer management.
- Quotation management.
- Subscription lifecycle tracking.
- Invoice and recurring billing management.
- Discount management.
- Payment tracking.
- Reports and analytics.
